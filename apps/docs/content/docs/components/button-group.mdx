## Notes

Not part of the Figma kit. Because Nooxit buttons are pills, a group keeps the
pill ends on its outer buttons and the 6px radius on inputs and selects.

**Source** — [`src/components/button-group.tsx`](https://github.com/markokojicki98/nooxit-design-system/blob/main/packages/ui/src/components/button-group.tsx)\
